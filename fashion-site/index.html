03 — The House

A legacy of craftsmanship

Founded in a small atelier on Rue Saint-Honoré, Maison Aurèle has spent four decades perfecting the language of quiet elegance. Every garment passes through thirty-seven hands before it leaves our atelier — a ritual of patience we refuse to compromise.

We source only from mills that share our devotion to longevity: Italian wool, French silk, Scottish cashmere. Materials chosen not for trend, but for the decades they will endure.
